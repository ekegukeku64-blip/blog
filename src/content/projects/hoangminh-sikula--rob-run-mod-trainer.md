Rob Run Mod Trainer is a community-built toolkit that reshapes how you experience Rob Run, handing you a generous spread of trainer options and quality-of-life enhancements without touching the core thrill of the chase. Built for tinkerers and modders alike, it bundles a configurable aim assistant, detailed player and vehicle overlays, a tactical radar, and heist-aware markers into one cohesive, user-friendly package. Every module is designed to be tweaked, remixed, and extended, so you can dial in the exact experience you want and contribute your own improvements to the project. If you've ever wanted a trainer that treats you like a collaborator, this one is open and ready for your pull requests.

## 📋 System Requirements

- Windows 10 or Windows 11
- PowerShell 5.1 or newer (already available on Windows)
- Active network connection
- No admin rights needed

## Feature Arsenal

**Aim Suite** — weapon-category presets for sidearms, long guns, scatterguns, and everything between. Dial in field-of-view radius, smoothing, and max engagement range, then let the humanization curve keep your tracking looking organic. Bone-priority targeting, movement lead, and per-role filters (police, robbers, civilians, ambient NPCs) round out the package. Hotkeys for activation, forced aim, and rapid target cycling are all rebindable.

**Player Awareness** — 2D or 3D boxes, skeleton rigs, name tags, clan labels, equipped weapon readouts, health bars, and distance markers. Filter by role or vital state, fade boxes with range, and toggle enemy tracer lines. Teammates and downed targets can be hidden with a single flip.

**Ambient NPC Overlay** — name, state, and distance for every non-player character in the scene, so you always know who's a bystander and who's a threat.

**Vehicle Overlay** — name, owner, speed, occupancy status, and box rendering for every ride on the map. Hide your own vehicle or the one you're riding in to keep the view clean.

**Camera Overlay** — view-zone visualization with distance and color-coded condition indicators (normal, broken, alarm). Hide broken units if you only care about live surveillance.

**Loot Overlay** — categorized readouts for weapons, ammo, armor, gear, cash, crates, and bags. Rarity-tier filtering (uncommon and up, rare and up, epic and up, legendary) plus loot tracer lines and crate-content previews.

**Radar** — a compact player-and-vehicle tracker with adjustable position, scale, transparency, and range. Off-screen arrows keep you oriented when targets slip past the frame.

**Heist Overlay** — security panels, metal detectors, safes, and alarms highlighted in the world, with NPC alert-state and police-call progress tracking so you can read a room before you step into it.

**Weapon Tuning** — recoil and spread both fully neutralized through the modifier panel, applied per weapon category.

**Camera Tools** — a detached freecam (default F9) with WASD plus Space/Ctrl/Shift movement, a custom field-of-view slider spanning 40° to 140°, and a zoom toggle (default Z) for tight 5° to 55° framing.

**Configuration Hub** — save, load, rename, and delete profiles. UI scaling from 60% to 120%, a roster of built-in themes (Focus blue, Ice blue, Violet, Mint, Rose, Amber) plus a custom palette slot, and interface language options. Menu, unload, and loot-switch hotkeys are all remappable, so the whole arsenal bends to your muscle memory.

Everything above ships unlocked from the first launch — no paywalls, no tier gates, no activation rituals. Just extract, run, and start tuning.

## Field Manual: Installation & Setup

Before you can bend the streets of the city to your will, you need to get the trainer properly seated in your rig. Follow these steps in order — skipping ahead tends to cause headaches, and nobody wants a grumpy launcher.

**Step 1: Secure the Package**
Navigate to the Releases area of the Rob Run Mod Trainer project and pull down the most recent build archive. Only the official archive from the project itself is the genuine article — treat anything else with suspicion.

**Step 2: Unpack the Kit**
Extract the archive to a folder you'll remember. Avoid tucking it deep inside system directories; a clean, dedicated folder keeps things tidy and makes future updates painless.

**Step 3: Prepare the Battleground**
Fire up Rob Run first and set the game to Windowed or Borderless mode. Fullscreen exclusive can fight with the overlay, so give the trainer a window it can talk to.

**Step 4: Bring the Trainer Online**
Run the trainer executable with administrator privileges — right-click and choose the elevated launch option. Wait for the interface to settle before touching any toggles.

**Step 5: Sync the Experience**
Once both windows are alive, the trainer will link up with the running game session. The menu key (Insert by default) brings the control panel to the foreground whenever you need to adjust your loadout mid-run.

**Step 6: Trial Run**
Take a short stroll through a low-stakes area to confirm the overlay, toggles, and readouts respond as expected. If something feels off, close both applications and repeat from Step 3 — a fresh handshake solves most hiccups.

**Troubleshooting Notes**
- If the panel doesn't appear, confirm the game is running in the supported display mode and that the trainer launched with elevated rights.
- Antivirus software occasionally flags the trainer out of caution. Whitelist the folder if you trust the source — which you should, since you grabbed it straight from the project.
- Keep your build current. Older archives can drift out of sync with game updates and produce unexpected behavior.

With the kit installed and synced, the city is yours to explore on your own terms. Proceed to the Compatibility Matrix to confirm your setup is battle-ready, then dive into the Feature Arsenal and start tuning your experience.
